# Caribex-Exchange - Premium Footwear & Movement Shop

## Overview

**Caribex-Exchange** is a sophisticated, high-end e-commerce platform dedicated to performance-driven, comfortable, and stylish footwear. Designed for individuals who prioritize movement and clean aesthetics, the platform offers curated collections including Men’s fashion, Slip-Resistant workwear, and innovative footwear technology. The interface is crafted to provide a premium, seamless shopping experience with a focus on durability, balance, and modern design.

## Features

- **Responsive Design**: A flawless, adaptive experience across all screen sizes, from mobile devices to ultra-wide monitors.
- **Premium Aesthetics**: A luxurious design system featuring a refined palette (Earth tones, whites, and blacks) and modern typography.
- **Sophisticated Animations**: Immersive user interactions and scroll effects powered by **GSAP (GreenSock)** and **ScrollSmoother**.
- **Specialized Collections**: Dedicated sections for Men's fashion, Slip-Resistant professional shoes, and specific technological innovations.
- **Interactive Product Exploration**: High-quality imagery with **FancyBox** lightbox integration for detailed product viewing.
- **E-commerce Flow**: Full shopping journey including a product gallery, single product detail page, cart management, and a secure checkout interface.
- **User Portals**: Integrated account management with a sleek registration modal and login portal.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Frameworks**: Bootstrap 5, jQuery 3.6.0
- **Animations**: GSAP (GreenSock), ScrollMapper, WOW.js, Animate.css
- **Libraries**: Slick Carousel (Dynamic Sliders), FancyBox (Image Lightbox), Font Awesome 6.7.2 (Icons)
- **Fonts**: Google Fonts (Montserrat, Poppins, Roboto) and Custom Typography (Gondens)
